GIS thinking

Every plan is a stack of layers

Planning questions become clearer when terrain, land use, water, infrastructure and people are mapped on one shared base. That is the core of my workflow.

  • Land use/land cover maps for the Barind Tract groundwater studies
  • Digitized features and 2D/3D databases for the Nawabganj Upazila Development Plan
  • Drone-based geospatial data collection training with the RUET GIS Club
